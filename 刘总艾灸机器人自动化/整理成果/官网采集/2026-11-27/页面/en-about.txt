01 / COMPANY PROFILE
About CUCA
Making health tech more human.

We believe the future of wellness should be smarter, more accessible and more human.

Founded in Singapore in 2024, CUCA HEALTH & WELLNESS PTE. LTD. explores the intersection of artificial intelligence and wellness through robotics, health products and new service models.

Our focus combines AI, wellness, robotics and connected business platforms, bridging technology, products, physical locations and people to improve customer and partner experiences.

We value ongoing innovation, operational excellence and responsible partnerships while building practical, service-led capabilities across the region.
