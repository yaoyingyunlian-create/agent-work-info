Product Overview

The AI Moxa-Moxibustion Robot integrates cutting-edge

Artificial Intelligence and advanced robotics with the timeless principles of

Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). Featuring authentic open-flame moxibustion,

AI-powered replication of master techniques, intelligent treatment path

planning, automatic ash removal, and an efficient smoke purification system, it

seamlessly combines traditional healing with modern technology.

Designed for safety, precision, comfort, and consistency, it

delivers an intelligent moxibustion experience unlike any other.

Key Features

1. Authentic Open-Flame Moxibustion

Experience the True Warmth of Traditional Therapy

Utilizing genuine open-flame moxibustion, the robot

preserves the deep heat penetration and herbal efficacy of traditional moxa

treatment, delivering an authentic therapeutic experience.

2. AI Replicates Master-Level Techniques

Precision Like an Experienced Practitioner

Powered by deep learning algorithms, the robot accurately

reproduces the hand movements, treatment patterns, and application techniques

of experienced moxibustion experts, ensuring professional and consistent

therapy every session.

3. Intelligent Path Planning

Smart Navigation with Sensitive Area Protection

The AI system automatically plans the optimal treatment path

based on acupoint locations and body contours, intelligently avoiding sensitive

areas for safer and more efficient therapy.

4. Automatic Ash Removal & High-Efficiency Smoke

Purification

Cleaner Treatment Environment

The robot continuously removes moxa ash during operation

while its advanced smoke purification system effectively filters smoke and

odors, creating a cleaner, healthier, and more comfortable indoor environment.

5. Human–Robot Collaboration

Intelligent, Safe & Reliable

Equipped with multiple safety protection systems, the robot

continuously monitors temperature, positioning, and treatment status in real

time, ensuring every moxibustion session is safe, stable, and worry-free.
